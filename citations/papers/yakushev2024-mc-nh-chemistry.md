## TL;DR

Experimental and computational source for Mc/Nh gas-solid chromatography and relativistic-chemistry visualization.

## Relevance

Supports a chemistry education module comparing Mc/Nh adsorption behavior with lighter homologues and with relativistic DFT expectations.

## Key Facts

- The study produced `288Mc` via `48Ca` on `243Am` and investigated adsorption of `288Mc` and daughter `284Nh`.
- It reports silicon oxide and gold surface interactions using gas-solid chromatography and discusses relativistic valence-orbital effects.

## Limitations

Chemical reactivity results do not validate nuclear stability or propulsion claims. Use only for chemistry education and source-backed visualization.
