## TL;DR

Evaluated compilation of nuclide properties including mass excess, isomers, half-lives, spin/parity, decay modes, and bibliographic information.

## Relevance

Useful for Statera isotope cards, uncertainty labeling, and evaluated-vs-estimated data boundaries.

## Key Facts

- NUBASE2020 compiles and evaluates properties for ground and isomeric states.
- It flags non-experimental information inferred from trends, matching Statera's need to label model or estimate data clearly.

## Limitations

Imported NUBASE records need parser tests and explicit flag handling before simulation use.
