## TL;DR

Model-comparison source for alpha-decay Q-values and half-life formulas in superheavy nuclei.

## Relevance

Candidate source for Statera's proposed Alpha Systematics Lab. It supports model comparison and sensitivity visualization, not evaluated-data defaults.

## Key Facts

- The paper compares multiple mass models and alpha half-life formulas against experimental superheavy data.
- It states that alpha half-lives are very sensitive to Q_alpha values.

## Limitations

Formula outputs must be labeled theoretical or model-derived. The source compares models; it does not make a single evaluated-data recommendation for Statera.
