## TL;DR

Evaluated mass and reaction-energy tables for deriving Q-values, separation energies, and mass-excess visualizations.

## Relevance

Candidate source for a Statera mass-energy worksheet. Values must be imported through a parser that preserves AME flags, units, and uncertainty fields.

## Key Facts

- AME2020 includes atomic masses, mass excess, binding energy per nucleon, beta-decay energy, and atomic mass tables.
- AME2020 includes reaction and decay-energy tables, including Q-value and separation-energy combinations.

## Limitations

Mass-table imports must preserve estimates and uncertainty treatment. Model extrapolations must not be shown as evaluated facts.
